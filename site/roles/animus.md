# Animus

> *The Warp never rents a vessel cheaply.*

You are the cabal's summoner. Once per game, you can reach into a player you *believe* is drowning in the Warp — and if you're right, speak with their voice for a day. If you're wrong, nothing happens, and you'll never know why.

**Night action — Possess.** One target, once per game. No confirmation on failure.

| Action | Effect (target) | Cost (you) | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Possess** | If the target is in Red drift (15–19), you take over their day | **+3 drift, win or lose** | Once per game — the role is spent on first use |

**Drift cost (Warp invoice):** **+3, no matter what happens.** You pay the moment you submit the attempt, not on success. This is your only drift cost in the entire game.

**⚠️ You are guessing blind.** You cannot see anyone's drift number or zone. The only tells are what the table shows you — who's been climbing (wrong votes, wrong interrogations, sustained pressure). Guess a player in Red (15–19) and possession succeeds. Guess anyone else — Green, Yellow, Orange, or Black — and the attempt **wastes silently**. You will not learn their real zone. The cabal sees no result either way until the outcome is known.

**What you cannot target:**

- A fellow Heretic — rejected outright, no drift charged.
- A dead player.
- Someone already possessed.

**What happens on a successful possession:**

| Element | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Day chat** | You write their messages for the day. Everyone sees the target's name, not yours. |
| **Their vote** | Cast normally by their client, but silently doesn't count. No one is told it was suppressed. |
| **Their night action** | Skipped entirely for the following night. |
| **Their own chat** | Locked. They cannot post on their own behalf while possessed. |

**The reveal.** After the day's vote tally is announced — win or lose that vote — the possessed body **detonates**: killed, and revealed publicly with full role, faction, and drift, as a Neverborn. This fires *regardless* of that day's vote outcome, including if the possessed player was the one just lynched — the reveal still lands on the same body. All living players except you take +1 witnessed-violence drift.

**After a successful possession, you sit at Yellow (+3).** Not dangerous alone, but a T2+ Interrogator scan against you afterward auto-kills on sight, same as anyone else in range. You survive the possession itself — the next scan is the real risk.

**Counterplay against you:**

- **Interrogator scan or Priest heal on your target, the night before you act** — either drops them out of Red before you ever reach them. Possession fails and you never know why.
- **Saboteur trap on your target** — your +3 becomes +8 (trap's +5 on top), whether the guess was right or wrong.
- **Lynching you directly** — denies the cabal the role entirely. You never got to use it.

**Play this role if:** you're comfortable making one high-stakes read with zero confirmation, and you're willing to spend the game watching drift-pressure cues (wrong votes, wrong interrogations) instead of asking your cell for help. A correct guess buys the cabal a full day of untraceable chat and a silently-voided vote — a wrong one just quietly costs you +3 for nothing.

**Loyalists should look for:** who in the cabal never speaks during the day yet seems to know things — a possessed ally posting oddly, or a vote that clearly should have landed but somehow didn't. A body detonating at day's end with no lynch attached is the confirmation this role was in play.

**Heretics should look for:** whoever your cell already suspects of climbing toward Red (repeated wrong-lynch or wrong-interrogation drift) is your best real target — don't rely on hope. If the cabal's own Blood Ritual or sermon pressure is what's pushing someone toward Red, coordinate so the Animus fires on the same night that target is actually there, not a night early or late.

> **Custom roster only.** Animus never appears in a standard, randomly-assigned game — a host has to add it deliberately when building a custom roster (8p+ recommended, so a wrong guess doesn't cost the cabal too much of the table).
