# Sanctioned Violence

> *The cabbal strikes as one throat.*

You are a Heretic. You are not the Murderer (if your cell has one). But tonight, the cabbal has decided to *attack* — and they chose you to hold the blade.

**Sanctioned Violence is a faction-wide action.** Once per night, the cabbal picks one operative to carry the attack. That operative pays the drift cost personally. The attack has a two-step escalation that mirrors the day-phase: **cripple on the first night, kill on the second.**

**Night action — Attack.** One target. Step 1 = cripple, step 2 = kill.

| Action | Effect (target) | Cost (you) | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attack (cripple)** | Target's tier increases by 1 | **+3 drift** | One attack per night, faction-wide |
| **Attack (kill)** | Target's tier maxes → target dies | **+3 drift** | Consecutive night on the same target |

**Drift cost (Warp invoice):** **+3 per attack.** Mid-tier. A dedicated attacker sits at:

| Night | Attacker drift | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +3 | Green |
| 2 | +6 | Yellow |
| 3 | +9 | Yellow |
| 4 | +12 | Orange — **Execute on Sight** |
| 5 | +15 | Red |
| 6 | +18 | Red |
| 7 | +20 | Black |

**A dedicated attacker dies by Night 4 if they don't rotate.** The cabbal must rotate the attacker to keep any single Heretic from cliff-falling into Execute on Sight.

**The two-step escalation.** The attack mirrors the day phase exactly:

| Night N | Night N+1 (same target) | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Attack Player X | (different target, or skip) | **Cripple X** — tier +1, no death |
| Attack Player X | Attack Player X again | **Kill X** — tier to MAX + death + alignment revealed |
| Attack Player X | (X dies Night N → N+1) | Reset — X is no longer valid |
| Skip attack | anything | Reset — escalation broken |

**The day vote between the two consecutive nights is the Loyalist's only window.** If they don't lynch the attacker between the cripple and the kill, the kill resolves.

**Who attacks?** The faction chat (the night-only cabbal channel) is where the cell decides. Three patterns are common:

- **Rotation:** the cell spreads the cost across the cabbal. Conspirator, Saboteur, Recruiter in turn. No single Heretic climbs into Orange.
- **Dedicated attacker:** the cell picks the most expendable Heretic and burns them until they hit Red.
- **Recruiter-proxy:** the cell uses a *converted* Loyalist as the attacker. Once converted, they're at Black (drift 20) — they *can't* drift higher, so the gate doesn't apply to them. (Open Q36.)

**Counterplay for the cell:**

- **Chirurgeon protect** — silent. The target survives. The attacker paid +3 for nothing.
- **Arbitrator proxy** — the Arbitrator dies in the target's place. The attack still resolves.
- **Saboteur trap** — trap the suspected target. The attacker's +3 becomes +8 (Saboteur's +5 on top).

**Counterplay for the Loyalists:**

- **Day vote on the cripple night.** That's your one window.
- **Execute on Sight.** If the attacker climbs into Orange (drift ≥ 10), a T2 Interrogator scan auto-kills them mid-escalation.
- **Watch for drift-zone behavior.** A Heretic who climbs zones *and* is nightly active is a Sanctioned Violence attacker.

**Play this role if:** you want to be the cabbal's coordinated edge. The two-step escalation means the cabbal's aggression is *visible* — the table has a day to react. The Murderer is the trump card; Sanctioned Violence is the steady grind.

**Loyalists should look for:** the player who *never* acts during the day and whose drift is climbing through the zones. Pull them on a Tier 2 lynching day. Alternatively, an Interrogator T2 scan against an Orange+ suspect executes them on sight.

**Heretics should look for:** the cabbal's tempo decision. If your Priest is busy, you can't sustain a dedicated attacker. If your Priest is free, the cell can afford to rotate. If your Priest is *wasting* heals on the Murderer, the cabbal's Sanctioned Violence attackers will burn out fast.
