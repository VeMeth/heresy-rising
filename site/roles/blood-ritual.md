# Blood Ritual

> *The cabal strikes as one throat.*

You are a Heretic. You don't have to be the Murderer to draw blood tonight — **Blood Ritual is a faction-wide action any living, uncrippled Heretic can take**, including the Murderer themselves (though a Murderer who uses it that night gives up their own kill — the two share one action slot). Once per night, the cabal picks one operative to carry the attack. That operative pays the drift cost personally.

**Night action — Attack.** One target, faction-wide (first submission each night wins the slot). Cripple on the first hit, kill if you hit the *same* target again the very next night.

| Action | Effect (target) | Cost (you) | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attack (cripple)** | Target's cripple tier increases by 1 | **+3 drift** | One attack per night, faction-wide |
| **Attack (kill)** | Same target as the immediately preceding night — tier maxes to 3, target dies, alignment revealed | **+3 drift** | Requires the prior night's attack to have been a cripple (not a kill) on this exact target |

**Drift cost (Warp invoice):** **+3 per attack, every time — win, lose, or blocked.** A dedicated attacker who keeps taking the slot climbs fast:

| Night | Attacker drift | Zone |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | +3 | Green |
| 2 | +6 | Yellow |
| 3 | +9 | Yellow |
| 4 | +12 | Orange |
| 5 | +15 | Red — **Execute on Sight** |
| 6 | +18 | Red — Execute on Sight |
| 7 | +20 | Black — Execute on Sight |

**A dedicated attacker is exposed to Execute on Sight by Night 5** (Red, 15+) if they never rotate out. The cabal should spread the cost across whoever's available rather than burning one member down.

**The escalation resets easily.** Different target the next night, or a skipped night in between, and the streak is gone — the next hit is a fresh cripple, not a kill. Only two attacks on the *exact same* target on *consecutive* nights lands the kill.

**Who attacks?** The faction chat (night-only cabal channel) is where the cell decides. Three patterns are common:

- **Rotation:** the cell spreads the cost across the cabal — no single Heretic climbs into Red alone.
- **Dedicated attacker:** the cell picks the most expendable Heretic and burns them down deliberately.
- **The Murderer takes a night off their own kill to carry it instead** — useful when the Murderer's own drift is already gated and they'd rather not waste the night sleeping.

**Counterplay for the cell:**

- **Chirurgeon protect** — silent. The target survives. The attacker paid +3 for nothing.
- **Arbitrator proxy** — the Arbitrator dies in the target's place. The attack still resolves as a kill on the Arbitrator.
- **Saboteur trap** — trap the suspected target. The attacker's +3 becomes +8 (Saboteur's +5 on top).

**Counterplay for the Loyalists:**

- **The day vote is always live.** Unlike a two-day commitment, any day's vote that clears 60% of living players executes the target outright — you don't have to wait for a specific "cripple night" window to act.
- **Execute on Sight.** Once an attacker's drift reaches Red (15+), a T2+ Interrogator scan against them auto-kills, mid-escalation or not.
- **Watch for drift-zone behavior.** A Heretic who's nightly active and visibly climbing zones is a live suspect for this role.

**Play this role if:** you want to be the cabal's coordinated edge without needing the Murderer's drift-gate risk. The escalation is *visible* to the table — a crippled player one night is a loud tell — so the cabal's aggression always costs them some cover.

**Loyalists should look for:** a player crippled overnight with no lynch attached — that's the cripple half of the escalation, and the same target getting hit again the next night is the kill. An Interrogator T2 scan against an Orange+ suspect who keeps climbing is the structural counter once they cross into Red.

**Heretics should look for:** whether your Priest can spare a heal for whoever's carrying the attack that night. If your Priest is busy propping up the Murderer, your Blood Ritual attackers will burn out fast; if the cell rotates the slot, no one climbs high enough to matter.
