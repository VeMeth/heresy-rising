# Imperial Citizen

> *The Throne does not require you to be mighty. Only to be true.*

You have no night action. You cannot interrogate, protect, divine, or sermonize. What you have is a vote, two eyes, and the loyalty already written into your chest.

The Citadel is full of people exactly like you — that is its strength and its danger. Eleven conclaves, ten of them won by who noticed first.

**Night action:** None. Sleep. *(This is auto-applied — you cannot opt out, and you receive no feedback.)*

**Drift cost (Warp-corruption on your soul):** **−1 per night, auto-applied on sleep.** This is the *Citizens rest easy* bonus: a faithful operative who never acts nightly drifts slightly *cleaner* every round. You climb drift by **witnessing violence**, by **voting with the losing side**, by being on the wrong end of a Heretic Priest's sermon, **and by the Conclave Proximity Siphon** — every time a Conclave-list neighbor commits a night action that charges drift, you take **30% of that charge (floor 1)**. The Warp notices who was standing next to whom.

> **Conclave Proximity Siphon (v2.6.0).** Citizen takes `round(0.30 × actor_drift_cost)` from any Conclave-list neighbor's drift-charging night action, with a minimum of 1. The actor's nightly −1 sleep recovery is preserved and stacks with the siphon. See the Drift mechanic for the canonical table.

**Play this role if:** you want to be the Loyalist backbone — the one whose votes and observations tip the day. **Be aware:** adjacent to a Murderer, your drift will climb. The Warp notices the room geometry.

**Loyalists should look for:** Citizens are the tell. A Heretic cell rotates its night actions between Mine, Trap, Sermon, Catalyst, Forgery — *all* of those who don't match a Citizen claim. If someone claims Citizen and you find them posting nightly, that is a confession.

**Heretics should look for:** Citizens are targets of opportunity — no power, no protection, no special value to the cell, *and* they vote. Killing one each night slowly strangles the day-game. **But:** a Citizen adjacent to your Murderer eats +3 drift per kill. Stack the kills near the same Citizen and you push them toward Yellow. Press hard enough and the Interrogator can read them with a Soft scan.
