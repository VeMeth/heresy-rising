# Astropath

> *The warp remembers. It leaves a stain on every soul who passes through another's sight.*

You are the veiled Astropath — a psyker whose gift has been conditioned by Inquisitorial practice to read the residue of the warp itself. Each night, you pick one operative and one tier of reading, paying self-drift, to learn the names of all who targeted that operative on recent nights. You never learn *what* action they took, never their faction. Only names. Only visitors.

**Night action — Visitor History.** One target. One tier (T1, T2, or T3). The system reveals the names of players whose night actions targeted your chosen operative, across the nights and attribution detail your tier grants.

| Action | Effect | Cost (you) | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visitor History — T1** | Names of everyone who targeted the operative on the last fully-resolved night. | +3 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |
| **Visitor History — T2** | Names of everyone who targeted the operative across the last two nights, pooled (no per-night attribution). | +6 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |
| **Visitor History — T3** | Names of everyone who targeted the operative across the last two nights, WITH per-night attribution (who came which specific night). Flat +12 drift floor — never scales down at larger tables. | +12 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |

**Tier costs (Warp invoice):** +3 (T1), +6 (T2), or +12 (T3) per night you read. The T3 cost is a mandatory flat floor — it does not shrink on bigger rosters, unlike most tiered costs in this game.

**What the warp shows you:** the *names* of night actors who targeted your chosen operative. That is all. You see a list: "Player A targeted this operative on Night 2. Player B targeted them on Night 3. Player C targeted them both nights." Or at T1, just "Night 3: Player A, Player B, Player C targeted this operative."

**What you do *not* learn:** the target's role, faction, exact drift, *what action* they took, or when — only *who* came when (at T3) or just *who came* (at T1/T2).

**Cannot target self.** Self-read is not allowed. Reading your own warp-stain does not help the cell.

**When you are crippled** (cripple tier > 0), the engine silently rejects your action — no names, no drift cost, no error shown. This is the same silent-rejection convention as Chirurgeon, Arbitrator, and Novice-Psychic.

**Repeat targets allowed:** you can read the same operative on consecutive nights without penalty.

**Play this role if:** you want precise, high-confidence night-action history at a high self-cost, and you're comfortable never knowing *what* actions your targets took.

**Loyalists should look for:** if an Astropath reads an operative and names a Heretic as a visitor, the Loyalist cell now knows the Heretic acted *that specific night*. This is gold: it pins not just identity but a time and intent. An Astropath can turn a public accusation into a kill order by confirming the accused hit on a Loyalist.

**Heretics should look for:** the Astropath is expensive (12 drift floor at T3). They will run hot by Round 4 or 5 if they spam T3. Kill them early, or force them to drop to cheaper tiers and lose the per-night attribution that makes them valuable. If you know who they are, your night actions become propaganda — the Astropath will eventually name you visiting a Loyalist, and you can claim you were framed by the Interrogator's auto-kill mechanics.
