# Interrogator

> *Out of the thorn, the wine. Out of the suspect, the truth.*

You are the Inquisition's blade. Each night, you pick one operative and interrogate them at an intensity of your choice.

**Night action — Interrogate.** One target. One intensity. (Drift is the Warp's invoice — see the [How to Play primer](/how-to-play).)

| Action | Effect (target) | Cost (you) | Uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interrogate — T1 (Soft)** | Binary check: *Tainted* (yellow zone or above) or *Clean* (green). 70% true, 30% flipped. | +1 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |
| **Interrogate — T2 (Standard)** | The operative's drift **zone** (Green / Yellow / Orange / Red / Black). | +2 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |
| **Interrogate — T3 (Brutal)** | Confirmed faction: *Loyalist* or *Heretic*. No ambiguity. | +3 drift | Nightly, when not crippled |

The target's drift zone **auto-upgrades** the effective scan intensity:

| Target zone | Effective intensity |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | As-chosen |
| 🟡 Yellow | +1 effective |
| 🟠 Orange | +2 effective → T1 confirms, T2/T3 = auto-kill |
| 🔴 Red, ⬛ Black | +2 effective → T2/T3 = auto-kill |

**Execute on Sight:** if you hit T2 or higher against an Orange+ target, the operative is killed and their alignment revealed publicly. This is the *why* of nightly T1 spam — a Red-zone Heretic is exposed by a cheap scan and you pay only +1.

**Clearing a false interrogation mark.** The day-vote's two-interrogation rule (see [How to Play](/how-to-play)) can put an innocent Loyalist one probe away from execution. Your **T1** scan can intervene, using the target's *true* zone — not the noisy Clean/Tainted line above, which still flips 30% of the time:

| Target's true zone | Effect on an existing mark |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | **Cleared.** Your read is treated as definitive — the target is absolved. |
| 🟡 Yellow / 🟠 Orange | **Unchanged.** Too noisy to make a binding call. |
| 🔴 Red | **Set (or reinforced).** Even an *unmarked* target is marked by this alone — their very next day-vote interrogation is fatal. This is how a real scan gets weaponized. |
| ⬛ Black | **Moot.** Left untouched either way. |

This is public in effect, if not in name: everyone can already see who's marked (the table's shared at-risk list), so a mark quietly disappearing — or a previously-clean player suddenly appearing on it — is visible to anyone paying attention, even without a name attached to *why*.

**Loyalists should look for:** the Interrogator who never sleeps. If they hit T3 every night, they hit Black zone by Round 4 and *they* become the auto-kill target.

**Heretics should look for:** an Interrogator who is asking too many questions of the same person. That is a tell.
