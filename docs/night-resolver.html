Night Resolver
Order of Resolution

Every night collapses in one synchronous pass over fifteen stations. Order is the mechanic: what a station reads has already been written by the stations above it, and nothing below it can reach back. This is the map of that ordering, drawn from the engine rather than the specs.

Zones are read live, mid-resolution. A station that moves drift can move a target across a zone boundary before a later station checks that zone — the whole reason the ordering below is load-bearing.

The Execution Rail fifteen stations, four shared buckets

The resolver carries four pieces of mutable state from station to station. A filled square is where a bucket is written; a hollow circle is where a later station reads it. The vertical distance between a write and its reads is the counterplay window.

Attack Funnel stations 8 and 9, gate by gate

Kill and Blood Ritual run the same interrupt chain in the same order. A solid exit ends the attack; a dashed exit charges drift and lets it continue.

Station by Station exact behaviour at each stop

0
Build night state setup
  • Snapshots the alive roster. This snapshot is reused later for Execute-on-Sight witnesses and for the sleep report, and is never refreshed.
  • totalPlayers is the full roster including the dead — scaled ability costs are priced against table size at setup, not the survivors.
  • Every boobytrap action is indexed by its target, not its actor. "Trapped" always means "someone laid a trap on this player".
1
Sleep & flat action costs every living player, in seat order
  • No action submitted → −1 drift.
  • kill, blood-ritual, possess, sermon, corrupt-sermon are skipped here and charged in their own stations.
  • Everything else pays now: a scaled cost for the Interrogator and Astropath, otherwise the flat driftWeight. Anything above zero enters nightCharges.
  • Interrogator / Priest / Heretic Priest scale on the standard curve — T1 2→1, T2 4→2, T3 10→4 from a 5-player to a 12-player table. The Astropath deliberately does not: 3 / 6 / 12 flat at every table size.
edgeskip_next_night is cleared here and the player is skipped — but nothing anywhere blocks them from submitting. A tortured or possessed player who submits still has that action resolved in full at every station below, and pays no cost for it.
2
Protect Chirurgeon · driftWeight 1
  • A trap on the target cancels it outright: Chirurgeon takes +5, the trapper is told who they caught.
  • On landing, the target enters protectedIds and is silently cured of plague — carrier flag cleared, Patient Zero nulled if that was them, and a tier-1 cripple lifted. Tier 2+ is never touched.
  • The cure runs before the infect and plague stations, so it lifts what a target already carries but cannot pre-empt tonight's infection.
  • The Chirurgeon is told nothing about any of it — same line whether the protect fired, cured, or was wasted.
3
Bodyguard Arbitrator · driftWeight 1
  • Trap on the target cancels it. Otherwise registers bodyguards[target] = guard.
  • Read only by the Kill and Blood Ritual stations — a bodyguard does nothing against a Catalyst or a Possession.
4
Infect Poxwalker · one per game
  • The one shot is burned on submission regardless of outcome — a trapped infect still spends it.
  • Sets patient_zero in the database and in memory, so the plague station reads it the same pass.
  • Self-cost of +3 was already charged at station 1.
5
Sermons Priest · Heretic Priest
  • Trap on the target cancels it.
  • Loyalist: whisper −2, hymn −5 (2×), litany −10 (1×). Heretic: false comfort +3, twisted hymn +7, warp litany +12 (1×).
  • Warp Litany fizzles silently if the target is below drift 10 — no self-cost, no target drift, no usage burned, no message to either side. Deliberate: the gate at submission time would have leaked hidden drift.
  • Self-cost is the scaled curve, not the self value in drift.json — that field is a dead fallback for both current sermon roles.
6
Intel & Execute on Sight Interrogator · Novice Psychic · Astropath
  • Trap on the target cancels the scan.
  • Effective tier = min(3, intensity + zoneUpgrade), where Yellow/Orange add 1 and Red/Black add 2.
  • Execute on Sight fires at intensity ≥ 2 against Red or Black: the target dies on the spot, alignment announced publicly, and every living player takes +1 witnessed violence.
  • A T1 read is 70% truthful, and separately can set or lift a torture mark off the target's true zone — Green absolves, Red marks them for execution at their next day-vote torture.
  • Astropath warp-reads name visitors from previous nights only; tonight's actions are not history yet.
edgeThe witness list here is the station-0 snapshot, so a player already killed earlier this same night still takes the +1.
7
Heretical Catalyst Recruiter · driftWeight 3
  • Converts the target to Heretic only if their drift is at 20 and they are not protected. Their plague is cleared on conversion.
  • A trap on the target taxes +5 but does not stop the conversion — one of only two stations a trap cannot cancel.
  • Because it sits above Kill, Blood Ritual and Possess, it reads drift that stations 5 and 6 have already moved but nothing below has.
edgeThis station inlines its trap tax instead of calling the shared helper, so the Saboteur receives no "your trap caught someone" message here. It is the only station where a sprung trap is silent to its owner.
8
Kill Murderer · Sanctioned Psyker · driftWeight 15
  • Murderer gate: if drift + 15 > 20 the kill fizzles — no self-cost, target takes +1, actor gets a zone-flavoured cue. In practice: a Murderer above drift 5 can never kill again.
  • Otherwise +15 self-drift is charged and enters nightCharges.
  • Trap on actor → +5, kill cancelled (cost already paid). Trap on target → +5, kill continues.
  • Bodyguard redirect is checked before protect. A landing kill announces publicly and gives every living player +1.
  • The Sanctioned Psyker's kill is once per game; the Murderer's is unlimited but gated.
9
Blood Ritual any Heretic · once per night · +3
  • Two-step escalation: cripples on first use, kills only if last round's ritual crippled this same target and they are still alive. Any kill-stage attempt, landed or blocked, consumes the streak.
  • Cripple raises the tier by one (cap 3); a tier-1 cripple is dated round+1 so it survives tonight's day transition.
  • Same bodyguard and protect interrupts as a kill, and the same target-trap tax.
  • An actor-trap does nothing here. Unlike a kill, the Blood Ritual station never checks whether the attacker is trapped.
10
Possess Animus · one per game
  • Runs last of the action stations by design — the target's zone reflects every sermon, kill, ritual and catalyst that happened tonight.
  • Self-drift +3 was charged at submission, not here; this station only registers it into nightCharges for the siphon.
  • Succeeds only if the target is in Red (15–19) and not protected. Black does not count.
  • Trap on actor → +5 and cancelled, the one shot still burned. Trap on target → +5, continues.
  • A protected target and a wrong-zone guess produce the identical failure message. The Animus never learns why.
  • Success sets skip_next_night and hands the Animus the target's day chat and vote. The body detonates at the end of that day — in resolveDay, not here.
11
Plague tick contagion · after every action has paid
  • While Patient Zero lives: everyone who targeted them tonight, and everyone they targeted, becomes a permanent carrier. The infect action itself is excluded, so the Poxwalker never catches their own plague.
  • Patient Zero takes +2; every other living carrier takes +1. Carrier marking is cumulative and never re-derived — losing the source stops new infections but not existing ones.
  • Then, for every infected player at drift 20 with no existing cripple: a 50% roll applies a one-night tier-1 cripple. Re-rolled every night. Never kills, never stacks.
  • Plague drift is deliberately kept out of nightCharges — the siphon prices actions, and the plague is not one.
12
Proximity Siphon Imperial Citizen
  • Each living Citizen looks at their two seat-adjacent neighbours and pays max(1, round(0.30 × charge)) for each one that spent drift on a night action.
  • Both neighbours can charge in the same night, and the results add.
  • A neighbour on the standard curve costs the Citizen the floor of 1; a Murderer's 15 costs them 5.
edgeAdjacency is position in the seat-ordered list, and the dead keep their slot. A corpse beside a Citizen permanently deadens that side rather than shifting the siphon to the next living seat.
13
Night report private, outcome-blind
  • One private line per raw submitted action — protect, bodyguard, trap, sermon, kill, infect. Identical whether it landed, was blocked, was redirected, or fizzled.
  • Catalyst, possess and the intel kinds are excluded: the first tells its actor nothing at all, the rest already carry their own message.
  • "You slept" only for players who submitted nothing and were not skipped — a skipped player gets no line rather than a false one.
14
Win check → Day n+1 phase transition
  • Heretic parity or Loyalist victory ends the game here; otherwise the phase advances to day, round n+1.
  • The transition clears any tier-1 cripple whose expiry is below the new round — which is exactly why night-set cripples are dated round+1 and day-set ones are dated round.
  • Day 1 has no vote; voting opens from round 2.

What the Ordering Buys consequences that only exist because of position

Sermon → Catalyst is live; Kill → Catalyst is not

The Catalyst at station 7 reads drift after the sermons (5) and after Execute-on-Sight's witnessed violence (6), but before any kill, ritual or possession. A Heretic Priest can warp-litany a target to 20 and have the Recruiter convert them the same night — but a Murderer's kill, which also spreads +1 to every witness, lands too late to help.

The Animus sees the whole night

Possession is last on purpose. Every drift change made tonight — sermons, witnessed violence from up to two separate deaths, plague ticks are the only thing it misses — is already applied when the target's zone is checked. A target read as Orange at dusk can genuinely be Red by the time the Animus reaches them.

Doubling defences is strictly worse

Bodyguard is checked before protect, and once a guard claims the hit the protect is never consulted. Chirurgeon and Arbitrator both covering the same player costs the table a dead Arbitrator and a wasted Chirurgeon night, where either alone would have cost nothing.

The Murderer prices itself out

Fifteen self-drift against a cap of twenty means the gate closes above drift 5. Every kill also feeds +1 back to the killer as a witness, and the heretic cap of 13 doesn't help — the gate is measured against max_drift, not the cap. A Murderer realistically gets one kill unless a sermon walks their drift back down.

Scaled costs never notice the dead

Ability scaling is priced against the full roster including corpses, so an Interrogator's T3 stays at its 12-player price for the entire game even when four players are left. Deliberate, per the Q31 ruling — but it means late-game scans are cheap relative to the surviving table.

nightCharges is a public-ish signal

Only drift-spending actions enter the bucket, so the siphon quietly tells an Imperial Citizen that a neighbour acted, and roughly how expensively. Plague drift is kept out precisely so it cannot be read the same way.

Sharp Edges found in the engine, not in any spec

Skipped players can still act, for free

Station 1 clears skip_next_night and skips the player's cost — but submitAction never checks the flag. A tortured or possessed player who submits a night action has it resolved at every station below and pays nothing for it. This is the most exploitable ordering consequence on the page.

Corpses witness executions

Execute-on-Sight distributes witnessed violence over the station-0 snapshot, while the kill station re-queries the living. Someone killed earlier in the same night still accrues +1 from an execution that follows.

A trapped Recruiter is invisible to the trapper

The Catalyst station inlines its +5 instead of calling the shared trap helper, so the Saboteur never learns their trap fired. Every other station tells them.

Blood Ritual ignores actor-traps

A trapped attacker loses a kill but not a ritual — the Blood Ritual station only checks the target. The in-code comment justifies this by claiming a trapped Heretic cannot submit, which is not how traps work; they resolve at night-end and never touch submission.

Dead seats deaden the siphon

Neighbour lookup is array-index adjacency over the seat-ordered roster with the dead left in place. An Imperial Citizen sitting next to a corpse loses that side of the siphon permanently rather than inheriting the next living neighbour.
